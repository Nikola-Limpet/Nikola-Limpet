# Continuous body backdrop

Generated with the built-in image generation tool using the full-page observatory concept as a reference. The production file is `assets/observatory-body-background.png`; `scripts/render-observatory-body.py` composes the editable vector content over it for desktop and mobile.

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: production background texture for the BODY of the supplied developer profile mockup, portrait 2:3 canvas.
Reference image: supplied image is a visual reference. Generate only a clean atmospheric background, with absolutely no text, letters, logos, icons, UI panels, lines, badges, animals, people, statistics or borders.
Primary request: recreate the dark atmospheric background which continues down the middle of that profile: deep ink navy almost-black center, subtle painterly indigo and violet nebula mist along left and right margins, scattered pinprick stars concentrated on outer edges, tiny distant blue mountains along the bottom, delicate dark pine and leafy silhouettes creeping in from the extreme lower corners. The central 80 percent is very dark and calm to place readable content over it. Avoid a large moon or bright celestial object, this is the middle body background not a hero. Keep subtle irregular texture throughout rather than a flat fill. Edge decoration must remain visible but restrained. Beautiful quiet nocturnal atmosphere coherent with reference. No central scenery, no horizon through the middle, no gradient cards. Fill entire canvas.
```
